# AI Workflow Lab
### Practical, human-reviewed AI workflows for everyday operations

A documented space for evaluating where AI can help with research, drafting, summarisation and routine coordination—and where human judgement must remain in control.

## Working principles

- Start with a clear business outcome, not a fashionable tool.
- Use only information approved for the selected AI service.
- Minimise or redact personal and confidential information.
- Treat outputs as drafts until reviewed.
- Verify names, dates, numbers, facts, sources and commitments.
- Keep a manual fallback for important workflows.
- Record limitations and test failures, not just successful examples.

## Workflow catalogue

| Workflow | Useful AI role | Human responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Meeting notes to actions | Structure notes into decisions, owners and due dates | Confirm accuracy and commitments |
| Research brief | Organise source material and draft a summary | Check sources, dates and factual claims |
| Email first draft | Produce a tone-adjusted starting point | Verify context, recipient and promises |
| SOP first draft | Convert approved process notes into a structured procedure | Validate steps, roles and policy |
| Weekly operations summary | Group completed work, blockers and open actions | Confirm source records and priorities |

## Example prompt patterns

### Meeting notes to action list

> Using only the meeting notes provided, extract (1) decisions explicitly made, (2) actions explicitly agreed, (3) named owners, (4) stated due dates and (5) unresolved questions. Do not invent owners or dates. Mark missing information as “not specified”. Return a concise table and a short list of items requiring human confirmation.

### Research brief

> Create a structured research brief from the supplied sources. Separate confirmed facts from interpretation, include the source and publication date for each material claim, flag conflicting information, and list questions that the sources do not answer. Do not fill gaps with guesses.

### SOP draft

> Turn the approved process notes into a draft SOP with purpose, scope, roles, prerequisites, numbered steps, exception handling, quality checks and revision history. Do not create policies or approval rules that are not present in the source notes. Mark gaps for the process owner.

These are starting prompts, not guarantees of accuracy. Test them with non-sensitive examples first.

## Evaluation checklist

Use the [AI Workflow Review](../operations-automation-toolkit/templates/ai-workflow-review.md) before introducing an AI-assisted process.

Evaluate:
- output accuracy and completeness;
- handling of missing or conflicting information;
- privacy and data governance;
- human review effort;
- repeatability across representative examples;
- failure handling and manual fallback;
- time saved versus time spent correcting outputs.

## Safety and privacy

Do not paste passwords, API keys, identity documents, private client records, financial information or confidential organisational material into an AI tool unless its use has been explicitly approved and appropriate safeguards are in place. Do not use AI output as the sole basis for consequential decisions.
