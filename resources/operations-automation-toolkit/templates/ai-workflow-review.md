## 2. Suitability
- [ ] The task is sufficiently clear and repeatable.
- [ ] The source information is accurate and authorised for use.
- [ ] The output can be checked by a person.
- [ ] Errors can be detected before they cause harm.
- [ ] There is a non-AI fallback process.

## 3. Data and privacy
- Data categories involved:
- Is personal or confidential data present?
- Has the organisation approved the tool and its data handling?
- Are retention, access and sharing settings understood?
- Can the task be completed using redacted or synthetic examples?

Do not submit passwords, payment details, sensitive personal data, confidential client records or internal documents to an AI service unless the organisation has explicitly approved that use and the necessary safeguards are in place.
