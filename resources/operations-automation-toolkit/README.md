# Operations & Automation Toolkit

A practical, evolving collection of reusable resources for founder support, executive assistance and digital operations.

The aim is simple: make recurring work easier to understand, coordinate and improve—without adding unnecessary complexity.

## What is included?

### 1. Daily operations brief

[Open the daily operations brief template](./templates/daily-operations-brief.md)

A lightweight structure for reviewing priorities, meetings, pending decisions and follow-ups.

### 2. Meeting-to-action workflow

[Open the meeting-to-action template](./templates/meeting-to-action.md)

A repeatable process for turning meeting notes into clear decisions, owners, due dates and follow-up actions.

### 3. Follow-up tracker

[Open the follow-up tracker CSV](./templates/follow-up-tracker.csv)

A simple register for tracking who needs a response, what is outstanding, the next action and when to revisit it.

### 4. Process documentation checklist

[Open the process documentation checklist](./templates/process-documentation-checklist.md)

A practical checklist for documenting a recurring workflow so another person can understand and follow it.

### 5. AI-assisted workflow review

A human-reviewed approach to using AI for summarisation, first drafts, research organisation and routine documentation. Sensitive information should not be entered into AI tools without appropriate authorisation and safeguards.

## How should these resources be used?

1. Start with the actual workflow and desired outcome.
2. Remove unnecessary steps before automating.
3. Assign a clear owner to each action.
4. Keep source records accurate and access appropriately restricted.
5. Review the process after real use and adapt it to the team.

## What is the status?

This is an evolving resource collection. Templates are being added and reviewed over time. It is not a packaged SaaS product, and no integrations or automated execution are implied unless explicitly documented.

## Who is it for?

- Founders and small teams
- Executive and virtual assistants
- Operations coordinators
- Small digital agencies
- People improving recurring administrative workflows
